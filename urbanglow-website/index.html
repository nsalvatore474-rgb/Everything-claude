About Urbanglow.ng

Real Products. Real Glow. No Compromise.

Urbanglow.ng was founded in Warri with one promise: every product we sell is 100% original and authentic, sourced directly from trusted brands and distributors — never counterfeits, never repackaged fakes.

We know the Nigerian skincare market is flooded with imitations. That's why we built a brand people can verify, trust, and reorder from with confidence. From serums to sunscreen, what you see is exactly what you get.

  • 100% original & authentic — always
  • Sealed products, never opened or diluted
  • Fast, reliable delivery across Nigeria
  • Friendly support on WhatsApp, before & after you buy
